Sectors

Public administration

Size plays no role here. State administration bodies are classified as essential entities regardless of size, as are operators of the state information infrastructure. Local and regional self-government units are classified as important entities.

Annex to the ActAnnex I - high-criticality sector
Competent CSIRTNational Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-HR)

The measures that carry most of the work in this sector

01Commitment and accountability
The contact person must be an official or executive body member, not from IT.
02Asset management
Data delivery to NCSC-HR also requires the IP address ranges the entity uses.
04Digital identities
Large user numbers, frequent role changes and long-lived access rights.
11Incident handling
Access to the PiXi platform runs through the national identification system and must be sorted before an incident.

What we most often find

  • A contact person appointed from IT instead of the management body
  • An incomplete list of the IP ranges the body uses
  • Access rights that persist after an internal transfer
  • Nobody has access to the incident reporting platform
