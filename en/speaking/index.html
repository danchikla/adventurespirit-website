Audiences

Board and supervisory board
What the rules require of you personally, which decisions you must take and what you are signing. No technical vocabulary. Usually 60 to 90 minutes.
IT and security
Measures, sub-measures, controls and evidence. How the scoring works and where organisations most often fail. Half a day or a full day.
All staff
Awareness training that does not teach people to spot phishing by its bad grammar. Short, concrete, with records that satisfy measure 5.
Conferences and professional events
Talks on the Cybersecurity Act, DORA, artificial intelligence and risk management.
