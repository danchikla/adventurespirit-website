Incident reporting deadline calculator

Deadlines under the Croatian Cybersecurity Act do not add up and do not run one after another. Enter the moment of awareness and the tool computes all five notifications with exact date and time.

Deadlines run from awareness, not from when the incident occurred and not from when the investigation ends.
If already sent, enter it. The final report deadline runs from it. Leave blank and it is computed from the 72-hour deadline.

The tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter reaches our server.

Your deadlines

Calculated from the moment of awareness

Notifications are submitted through the PiXi platform (pixi.carnet.hr), accessed via the national identification system. If the platform is unavailable, the notification is sent by form to the competent CSIRT and entered into the platform afterwards. CSIRT competence follows the sector, under Annex III of the Act.
