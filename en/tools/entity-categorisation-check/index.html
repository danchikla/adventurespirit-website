Entity categorisation check under the Croatian Cybersecurity Act

Your category is determined by the competent authority, which notifies you in writing. Until then it helps to know what is likely. This tool applies the criteria in Articles 9 to 15 of the Act and shows which rule decided.

Select the sector you operate in. If you carry out several activities, the one covered by the annexes governs.
Under Article 15 the annual average headcount and the total annual turnover or total assets are taken into account.
Tick everything that applies. Each of these overrides the size criterion.

The tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter reaches our server.
