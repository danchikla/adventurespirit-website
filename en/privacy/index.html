5 Cookieless visit statistics

We keep visit statistics using our own solution on our own server. We do not use Google Analytics or any other external measurement tool, so your data never leaves our infrastructure.

For this processing we set no cookies and store nothing on your device. The consent requirement under the electronic communications rules therefore does not apply, and the legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Why an individual cannot be tracked

We do not store your IP address. It is used only at the moment of the visit, as input to a cryptographic hash function together with a secret value and browser data. The result is a 16-character string from which the IP address cannot be reconstructed.

The secret value changes every day at midnight UTC and the old one is deleted. The same visitor therefore receives a completely different fingerprint the next day, so visits cannot be linked across days or attributed to a particular person. The result is aggregate statistics, not a profile.

We respect the Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control settings in your browser. If either is enabled, we record nothing. No statistics are collected if you disable JavaScript either.

Records are kept in monthly files on our server, are not publicly accessible and are not shared with third parties.

8 Your rights as a data subject

Under the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) you have the following rights:

  • Access (Art. 15) - you may request a copy of the personal data we process about you
  • Rectification (Art. 16) - you may request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (Art. 17) - you may request deletion of your data, the so-called right to be forgotten
  • Portability (Art. 20) - you may request your data in a machine-readable format (JSON or CSV)
  • Objection (Art. 21) - you may object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Restriction (Art. 18) - in certain cases you may request that processing be suspended
  • Withdrawal of consent - at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand

Send requests to info@adventurespirit.hr marked "GDPR request". We reply within 30 days of receipt (Art. 12(3) GDPR).

If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority:

  • AZOP - Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency, azop.hr

9 Data security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the state of the art:

  • HTTPS encryption of all communication (TLS 1.2 and above)
  • Password hashing (bcrypt or PBKDF2)
  • Role-based access control

In the event of a breach presenting a high risk to data subjects, we will notify you without undue delay in accordance with Art. 34 GDPR.
